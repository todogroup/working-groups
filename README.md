## 🌱 What is a TODO Working Group?

Within TODO, **working groups (WGs)** are the spaces where the community collaboratively builds frameworks, books, guides, metrics, and tooling for open source and AI management and oprerations. Working groups are:

- **Open to everyone.** You don't need to be a Linux Foundation or TODO Group member to participate. Just join the channel, the call, or open a pull request.
- **Community-driven.** Direction is set by the participants. The [Steering Committee](https://github.com/todogroup/governance#-steering-committee-members) approves the formation of new working groups and provides scaffolding to keep them sustainable, but it does not run them.
- **Public.** Calls follow [Chatham House Rules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chatham_House_Rule), meeting agendas are tracked in GitHub Discussions, and deliverables are released under permissive licenses (CC-BY 4.0 for content,Apache 2.0 for code).
  
> If you're new to the TODO Group, start with the [community onboarding guide](https://todogroup.org/community/get-started/) and the [TODO Charter](https://github.com/todogroup/governance/blob/main/CHARTER.adoc).

## 🔍 Working group details

### 📖 OSPO Book WG

**Mission.** Maintain and expand the [OSPO Book](https://ospobook.todogroup.org/),
a collaboratively authored reference for organizations developing
strategies to *use*, *contribute to*, or *create* open source projects
through Open Source Program Offices.

**Status.** Active development (v1.0).

**Where the work happens.**

- 📂 Working repo: [`todogroup/ospology/ospo-book`](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/tree/main/ospo-book)
- 🌐 Published book: <https://ospobook.todogroup.org/>

**Communication channels.**

- 💬 Slack: `#project-ospobook` (in the [TODO Slack](https://slack.todogroup.org))
- 📧 Mailing list: <https://lists.todogroup.org/g/WG-ospo-book-project>
- 📅 Meetings: see the [TODO Group Community Calendar](https://todogroup.org/community/meetings/)

**How to contribute.** Read the
[contribution guidelines in the working repo](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/tree/main/ospo-book#contributing)
and open a PR or issue. New chapters are proposed via
[GitHub Discussions in `ospology`](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/discussions)
under the `Ideas` or `proposal` labels.

### 🧩 OSS in Business WG

**Mission.** Produce the **OSS in Business Guide** — a vendor-neutral
resource that articulates the business value of open source and helps
organizations connect open source activity to strategic outcomes:
innovation, risk management, cost efficiency, and long-term
sustainability. The guide is aimed at decision-makers, OSPO
practitioners, and stakeholders outside engineering.

**Status.** Draft, active development.

**Where the work happens.**

- 📂 Working repo: [`todogroup/ospology/whitepapers/business-value`](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology/tree/main/whitepapers/business-value)

**Communication channels.**

- 💬 Slack: `#wg-oss-in-business`
- 📧 Mailing list: <https://lists.todogroup.org/g/WG-oss-in-business>
- 📅 Bi-weekly call — see the [TODO Group Community Calendar](https://todogroup.org/community/meetings/)

**How to contribute.** Pick up an open workstream in the working folder,
introduce yourself in `#wg-oss-in-business`, and join the bi-weekly
call. PRs against the whitepaper outline are welcome from anyone.


### 📊 CHAOSS OSPO Metrics WG

**Mission.** Advance how organizations understand and communicate the
value of open source projects and the value of the programs and
initiatives that support them. The WG defines metrics, metrics models,
and best-practice guidance for OSPOs to demonstrate impact.

This is a **joint working group** between the
[CHAOSS Project](https://chaoss.community/) and the TODO Group, with
co-chairs from both communities.

**Status.** Active. Co-chairs: Gary White and Dawn Foster.

**Where the work happens.**

- 📂 Working repo: [`chaoss/wg-ospo`](https://github.com/chaoss/wg-ospo)
- 🌐 CHAOSS metrics catalog: <https://chaoss.community/kbtopic/all-metrics/>
- 🧭 CHAOSS WG overview: <https://chaoss.community/kb/working-groups/>

**Communication channels.**

- 💬 Slack: `#wg-ospometrics-chaoss` (TODO Slack) and the CHAOSS Slack
- 📅 Bi-weekly call, Thursdays 11:00 US Central / 18:00 CET — see the
  [TODO Group Community Calendar](https://todogroup.org/community/meetings/)
  and the [CHAOSS calendar](https://chaoss.community/about-chaoss/governance/calendar/)

**How to contribute.** New metrics are proposed as PRs in
[`chaoss/wg-ospo`](https://github.com/chaoss/wg-ospo) following the
CHAOSS metric template. Discussion items are surfaced in either the
TODO or CHAOSS Slack and reviewed on the bi-weekly call.

## 🙌 How to join a working group

There is **no formal membership** for TODO working groups. To get involved:

1. **Join the [TODO Slack](https://slack.todogroup.org)** and the
   working-group-specific channel listed above.
2. **Subscribe** to the working group's mailing list (if one exists)
   on <https://lists.todogroup.org>.
3. **Add the meeting** to your calendar via the
   [TODO Group Community Calendar](https://todogroup.org/community/meetings/).
4. **Show up** to a call or open a PR — both count as participation.
5. **Sign your commits** with the
   [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/)
   (`git commit -s`). Required for all contributions to TODO repos.

> 💡 You don't need to be a TODO General Member or OSPO Associate to
> contribute. Working groups are open to all.


## 🛠 Working group lifecycle and inactivity policy

TODO has an inactivity policy for working groups, Slack channels, and mailing lists ([source: governance repo](https://github.com/todogroup/governance#-process-for-shutting-down-inactive-channels-or-working-groups)):

- **Phase 1 - 3 months of no activity.** A working group participant, chair or steering committee liaison notifies The TODO Project Manager of this inactivity, then posts a warning to the channel and mailing list and asks for volunteers to re-engage. 
- **Phase 2 - no volunteers after 2 weeks.** The PM archives the related materials, officially closes the WG, and notifies the Steering Committee. The closure is recorded as a GitHub Discussion for transparency. Archived materials remain available; nothing is deleted.
