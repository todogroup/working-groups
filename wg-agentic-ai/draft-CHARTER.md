# Agentic AI to Empower OSPOs Working Group Charter

## 🎯 Purpose

Explore, document, and share practical patterns for using **agentic AI** to scale Open Source and AI Management and Operations. The WG is a space for OSPO practitioners to compare notes, prototype together, and turn lessons
learned into resources the broader community can reuse.

## 🧭 Scope

**In scope**

- Agentic AI applied to core OSPO functions, such as license/compliance review, inbound and outbound request triage, project-health monitoring,
  internal Q&A over OSPO policy, contribution workflows, OSS metrics automation, advocacy and education content
- Patterns, prompts, skills, prototypes, evaluations, and post-mortems shared by practitioners
- The evolving *role* of the OSPO as organizations adopt AI

**Out of scope**

- Defining "open source AI" itself
- AI license/compliance frameworks at large
- Building or maintaining production AI tools

## 📦 Deliverables

The WG commits to three concrete outcomes:

2. **A paper on the role of OSPOs and AI** Articulates where the OSPO may sit in an organization's AI strategy and what new responsibilities are emerging
3. **A paper or blog post on how to use AI tools in an OSPO.** A playbook with concrete patterns, example agentic workflows, prompts, or lessons learned from OSPO practitioners running AI in production or in pilots

All deliverables will be published under CC-BY 4.0 via the [`todogroup/ospology`](https://github.com/todogroup/ospology) repo and the [TODO Group blog](https://todogroup.org/blog/).
