## Welcome to the Agentic AI to Empower OSPOs Working Group

The TODO Group Agentic AI to Empower OSPOs working group brings together OSPO practitioners and adjacent teams to explore how agentic AI can support open source and AI management and operations.
Through community discussions, practical show-and-tell sessions, and shared experimentation, the group examines emerging tools, use cases, risks, and reusable workflow patterns. Its goal is to turn practical experience into open resources that can help OSPOs understand, evaluate, and responsibly adopt agentic AI.
