## Open Source in Business Working Group

The Open Source in Business Working Group (OSS-in-Business WG) exists to fill a gap in the way organisations approach open source. 
While most guidance on open source is written from an engineering perspective, there is far less material that explains how it 
creates business value. The TODO Group launched this initiative through its Touchpoint calls to develop a collaborative guide that 
helps organisations understand how open source can drive business value.

The mission of the working group is to create a community-built reference that describes when and how open source should be 
incorporated into corporate strategies and business models, and to outline best practices for building sustainable open source 
ecosystems for business leaders.
